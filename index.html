Our first voyage

Northward

❧ ✦ ❧

Northward is an open-world co-op adventure about sailing a scrappy ship through a northern archipelago, exploring the uncharted, finding lost treasure, and uncovering the mystery of a fallen star.

Wishlist on Steam ↗ playnorthward.com Northward press kit

Explore a variety of islands and biomes, bustling harbors and deep waters. Survive the harsh climate and recruit new crew-members to help you on your journey. Upgrade your vessel, gather useful resources and equipment, and follow the clues that lead farther north.

Made by Yore & Yonder, Northward is a mix of mystery, cooperative exploration, and early-2000s animated-adventure spirit. With inspirations ranging from Outer Wilds and Sea of Thieves to Treasure Planet and Atlantis: The Lost Empire.

  • Sail a scrappy ship through a cold open-world, with up to 3 friends (4 total)
  • Explore 4 different biomes, and their unique challenges
  • Solve a non-linear mystery about a fallen star
  • Upgrade and customize your ship
  • Recruit characters you come across for your crew
  • Fishing and cooking by the campfire

A brief history of

The Studio

❧ ✦ ❧

We make the games we wish existed.

Yore & Yonder is the result of many evenings spent after work, toiling on the shared dream of two friends.

We met almost ten years ago at the University of Skövde in Sweden, where we both studied game development and programming. Over the years we worked on many smaller projects together, before deciding to take on the challenge of an ambitious game idea: one that would eventually become Northward. We have been working on it together since 2021, and officially founded Yore & Yonder in Stockholm in 2026. We also co-founded The Dev Cabinet, a Stockholm-based collective of game developers.

✦ · ✦ · ✦

Our philosophy is simple: if we are genuinely excited about something, chances are we are not alone. We question every design choice in the context of what we are making, instead of only following how it "should" be done. We put real care into our own tools and pipelines, because efficient workflows are what let creativity thrive.

To us, video games are a wonderful and unique art form that is still in its early days, and we are honoured to contribute to its story in whatever way we can.
